# E-Commerce Sales and Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive analysis of e-commerce sales data to identify key trends, consumer behavior, and profitability metrics. The primary objective is to provide actionable insights that can help an e-commerce business optimize its sales strategy, target the right audience, and improve overall revenue.

## 🎯 Project Objective
The goal of this analysis is to:
- Analyze sales performance across different months and states.
- Understand customer demographics (Age & Gender) and their impact on sales.
- Evaluate the performance of different sales channels (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.).
- Monitor order statuses and identify areas for logistics improvement.

## 📊 Key Insights & Findings
From the analysis of over **31,000 orders totaling ₹21.1 Million in sales**, the following insights were derived:

- **Gender Performance:** Women are the primary drivers of sales, contributing approximately **₹13.5M (64%)** compared to Men at **₹7.6M (36%)**.
- **Top Channels:** Amazon is the leading sales channel (**35%**), followed by Myntra (**23%**) and Flipkart (**21%**.
- **Geographic Distribution:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 performing states, contributing the highest revenue.
- **Age Demographics:** The Adult age group (**25–44**) is the most profitable segment, with women in this category being the largest customer base.
- **Order Health:** The majority of orders are successfully **Delivered (~92%)**, with a low combined rate of cancellations and returns.
- **Peak Sales:** Monthly data shows that **March** was the highest-performing month in terms of both order count and total amount.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, and Dashboard Creation
- **Data Visualization:** Bar Charts, Pie Charts, and Monthly Trend Lines

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset includes several key columns:

- **Order ID & Cust ID:** Unique identifiers for transactions and customers
- **Gender & Age Group:** Demographic information
- **Channel:** The platform where the sale occurred (Amazon, Myntra, etc.)
- **Category:** Product types (Kurta, Set, Top, etc.)
- **Amount & Qty:** Sales value and quantity
- **Ship-State:** Geographic location of the customer
- **Status:** Current state of the order (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned)
